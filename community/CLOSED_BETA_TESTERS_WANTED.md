# Ragutis Remote for STR — closed beta testers wanted

Hi everyone! 👋

I'd like to introduce **Ragutis Remote for SoundTouch Reborn**, an open-source Android companion app developed for the SoundTouch Reborn (STR) community.

The idea is simple: discover, manage and control STR-enabled SoundTouch speakers from an Android phone.

**Ragutis v0.3.5 is now available in a closed Google Play beta**, and I'm looking for community members who would like to help test it.

## What does Ragutis do?

- **Speaker discovery:** Find STR-enabled SoundTouch speakers on the local Wi-Fi network and reconnect to your last speaker.
- **Remote control:** Use the existing STR remote interface for playback, volume, presets and more.
- **Room management:** Save speakers, assign rooms and switch between devices.
- **Wi-Fi setup assistant:** Help supported SoundTouch speakers still running original Bose firmware connect to Wi-Fi before STR is installed.
- **German and English interface.**
- **Google Play updates** for the Play Store variant.

The Wi-Fi setup assistant has been tested with **SoundTouch 10 and SoundTouch 20 Series III** in specific configurations. Other SoundTouch models and firmware combinations are not yet confirmed.

**Requirements:** Android 8.0 or later for the remote; Android 10 or later for the optional Wi-Fi setup assistant. Remote control requires **SoundTouch Reborn already installed on the speaker** — Ragutis does **not** install STR.

## How to join the closed beta

### 1. Join the tester group

👉 **[Join Ragutis Closed Beta on Google Groups](https://groups.google.com/g/ragutis-closed-beta)**

Request membership if prompted. The closed test only allows accounts included in the linked tester group. If approval is required, continue after your membership has been accepted.

### 2. Opt in to the Google Play test

👉 **[Become a Ragutis beta tester](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/app.strremote.android)**

Open the link using the **same Google account** you used to join the group and use for Google Play. Select **Become a tester**.

### 3. Install or update Ragutis

👉 **[Install Ragutis from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.strremote.android)**

The closed beta is **v0.3.5**. If you already use a GitHub release, **please do not uninstall it first**. Installing the Play version over the existing installation lets us test whether saved speakers, rooms and settings survive the update. Back up any important setup details beforehand.

It may take some time for Google Play tester access to propagate after joining the group and opting in.

## What feedback would help?

I'm especially interested in:

- Speaker discovery, reconnection and connection reliability
- Playback and remote control, including presets
- Room assignments and switching between speakers
- Wi-Fi setup on additional SoundTouch models
- Compatibility with different Android versions
- Install/update behavior and preservation of existing settings
- Bugs, usability improvements and unexpected behavior

👉 **[Report bugs and suggestions in Ragutis GitHub Issues](https://github.com/K-Cosmo/ragutis-remote-for-str/issues)**

The app can also prepare an optional feedback email through your installed email app, so a GitHub account is not required to send feedback.

**If possible, please remain opted in for at least 14 consecutive days** to support Google's closed-test requirements, and genuinely try the app when you have time.
